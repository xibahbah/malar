Our story

Malar means flower.

We started with a table of earrings at a weekend market and a jar of flowers beside them. The flowers sold the earrings. Today malar is still small: a handful of makers, a studio that smells of clay and brass polish, and a promise that everything we sell was made by a person, not a line.
